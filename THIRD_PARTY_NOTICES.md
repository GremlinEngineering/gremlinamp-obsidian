# Third-party decoder notices

The Obsidian integration uses the GremlinAmp MIT license. The bundled decoder workers and WebAssembly modules retain their own licenses. The original notices are in `decoder-runtime/vendor` and are included in the manual installation archive. The Audacious source used for the PSF2 decoder, the adapter and its build recipe are in `decoder-source/psf2` and `tools/build-psf2.sh`.

Most decoder workers and binaries originate in the Gremlin music web player in `publisher-integration/gremlin-tg-publisher/games/music_player`. GremlinAmp's `decoder-sources` records the corresponding source archives and their provenance. See the [GremlinAmp decoder source notes](https://github.com/GremlinEngineering/gremlinamp/tree/main/decoder-sources). The website-only player, music catalog and tracks do not form part of this plugin.

PSF2 uses the Audacious 4.5 Audio Overload port. See the notices in the vendored sources and the [Audacious plugins source release](https://github.com/audacious-media-player/audacious-plugins/tree/audacious-plugins-4.5/src/psf).
